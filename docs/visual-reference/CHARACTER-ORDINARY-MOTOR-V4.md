## Predeclared lifecycle readout

For each actor, distinguish neutral-motor **decision checks**, eligible
checks and selected attempts from physical admissions, first certified paid
work, certified paid seconds, completed actions, and fixed interruption or
post-selection rejection buckets. Report unavailable terminal witnesses and
currently pending admissions separately. The observer reconciles unsaturated
session counters: selections equal admissions plus post-selection rejections;
admissions equal completed plus interrupted plus unavailable plus pending.
Completed motions require first payment and 1.8 certified seconds; total paid
seconds cannot exceed 1.8 times first-paid actions. A missing field fails the
projection rather than silently becoming zero. Saturated counters relax the
arithmetic only with an explicit saturation flag. Identity changes, local
session resets and `lastObservedPendingBeforeSessionLoss` bound what can be
inferred. That count means the **previous projection** saw a pending action
before an observed session boundary; it may have settled in between, and no
completion or interruption is inferred. Actions
that start and end between sparse projections contribute to cumulative
session counters, but their exact time and surrounding view may be unknown.

Count actor-local funded **own-pose sensor ticks** separately from partner
**peer-visible neutral-pose ticks** and generic pose/visible-absence/unknown
sight ticks. These are samples, not distinct gestures, action durations or
partner comprehension. A completed action may remain unseen by its partner;
zero peer-visible samples cannot by itself establish noncompletion. Count
memory retention, overflow and expiry and predictor forecasts, valid future
30-tick targets, pose/absence strata, onset/offset/stable transitions and
unknown/gap censors. Compare model, frozen-prior and persistence Brier and log
loss only over the same valid-target denominator, with class-conditional
counts. These are prequential training-stream scores, not held-out or causal
social learning. Censors and pending forecasts are not errors.

Keep the character-channel denominators distinct: optional reference checks,
feasible emissions, paid inspection/reply starts and completions, unique valid
listener demonstrations, replay uses, optimizer steps, sender feedback/updates
and censor stages. Critic accuracy applies only to completed demonstrations.
Report actual paid reply ambiguity without reviving historical reverse
preflight categories. Include health, water, energy intake/absorption,
expenditure, available/stored reserves, collapses, rescues and personally
supported sampling/drinking events as physical context. A selected or partial
motor action must not be charged an invented full-action cost.
