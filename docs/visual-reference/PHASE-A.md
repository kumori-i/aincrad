# Shared visual reference — physical verification stage

This branch's Phase A is **not a learned language**. It is the first verification
stage toward replacing delayed bodily-credit communication in the same main
Aincrad world. It must not be released as a completed learning milestone. No
associative memory, neural updates, imported weights or shared vocabulary exist
in this stage. Symbols and listener choices are arbitrary, separately randomized.
Historical protocol 1 and its wrapper remain unchanged.

## Supplied physical and sensory structure

- Ordinary safety, body-care and learned-procedure decisions remain prior to the
  new optional reference hook. There is one population and clock. Agents are not
  moved together, held idle or given food to make encounters happen.
- The candidate pair is chosen before a uniform referent draw. Each alternative
  must be mutually visible, distinguishable in both agents' existing 48 pooled
  RGB features (RMS distance at least .08), and uniquely resolvable by the same
  geometric rule. Pair, referent, candidate order, random choice and symbol draws
  use independent streams. No favorable redraw occurs after referent selection.
- The listener commits to an ordinary paid `inspect_object` action. Only its
  successful runtime canonical completion can issue a one-use indication permit.
  The sender must then reach its own ordinary optional decision slot.
- `indicate_object` costs exactly 1.8 simulated seconds at effort .25. It is not
  added to the random primitive menu. Private action funding and completion
  witnesses—not saved fields or displayed counters—establish paid completion.
- The public gesture is a **supplied schematic direction cue**, not an anatomical
  arm detector. A fixed 24-world-unit segment begins at the actor's ground-plane
  position. The renderer draws these exact world-coordinate endpoints after
  restoring the NPC sprite-local transform; normal camera transforms still apply.
  No target endpoint, ID or distance is exposed. No pose appears before funding.
- The parser extrapolates that short visible segment within a ±6° cone, at most
  400 units from its origin. That mathematical range is not a drawn beam, extra
  sight, or a requirement to see a 400-unit ray. Both actors retain their ordinary
  local range and line-of-sight rules; the whole short segment must be visible.
- The sensory universe is the existing supplied ecology-object projection, not
  all image pixels or independently learned scene segmentation. All currently
  visible projected objects participate as potential distractors, not just the
  two task choices. Because ordinary `senseEcology` caps its pre-LOS result at 96,
  exactly 96 is conservatively **unknown/incomplete**, and the interaction stops.
- A unique fresh listener-local resolution and fresh own crop must be continuously
  available over at least the final .5 seconds of consecutive sampled simulation
  intervals, using the main 1/60-second fixed step. The first valid sample is a
  baseline, not an observed interval; missing or partially unpaid intervals reset
  the measure. A materially partial-funded step receives no observation credit;
  sight is never backdated into an assumed funded prefix. This is sampled visibility, not a mathematical continuous proof. Render
  calls, wall time, critic answers and copied sender crops cannot count as sight.

A private transaction binds actual objects/actions, while the numeric parser sees
only public segment geometry and the listener's own visible positions. Reference
correctness is a separate observer critic. It is neither physiological reward nor
a teaching substitute. Movement, identity/epoch changes, urgent bodily needs,
ownership/visibility changes and interruptions censor transactions. A transaction
has one terminal owner; duplicate callbacks cannot double count or revive it.

## Limits and subsequent gates

Fixed thresholds, the parser, segmented crops, arbitrary alphabet and interaction
schedule are supplied experimental biases. They are not learned concepts,
creativity, English translations, grammar, or evidence of survival benefit.
The private memories proposed for Phase B must not be wired until exact-source
review, causal parser tests, canonical paid-action tests and actual native visual
proof clear this physical stage. Native screenshots alone do not prove perception.

No autonomous cohort has been run for this stage. A read-only elapsed-zero geometry
probe at the default positions and eight fixed translated position pairs found
5–9 unique and 2–6 ambiguous referents per direction, with no missing visible
objects due to the sensor cap. It tested neither pixel distinction, paid actions,
survival opportunities nor learning. Its settings were not retuned on outcomes.

Pending transactions and runtime permits do not survive reopening or world reset.
Save restoration cannot recreate executable authority. No user save is deleted.
The eventual association memory's reopen/reset contract requires its own Phase B
review; historical communication weights already restart on reopen.
