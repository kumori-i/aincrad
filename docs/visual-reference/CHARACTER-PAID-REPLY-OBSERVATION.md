# Paid character reply observation

This increment changes the **main sequence protocol** after a completed paid
inspection and sender indication. The listener keeps its one own-view training
example. The existing one-use response permit can then authorize one physical
reply pointing at the listener's original inspected choice. A hypothetical
sender-view parse no longer blocks that attempt. Historical legacy symbol
fixtures retain their prior preflight behavior.

The reply still needs an ordinary optional decision after survival priorities,
an intact actor and target, peer visibility, fresh sight, body funding, and the
unchanged 12-second transaction deadline. It takes 1.8 seconds of actual work
at 0.25 effort. Its public gesture exists only during funded work. The sender
observes that gesture through its own current view. A continuous final interval
of at least 0.5 seconds with one uniquely resolved object and a fresh sender
crop can yield the existing local visual match or mismatch. A continuously
ambiguous paid gesture is recorded as `response-ambiguous` only after successful
physical completion; it gives no sender reward, baseline change, or weight
update. Unseen, incomplete, interrupted, stale, gray-zone, and expired replies
also remain censored. The ambiguity interval exists only within the active
transaction and is not saved or an action permission.

The physical gesture and numeric pointing parser are supplied sensorimotor
scaffolding. The character string, listener choice, own-view demonstration,
and any sender update retain their separate bottom-up learning paths. This
slice supplies no repeat request, alternate gesture, new training label, or
new meaning for a word. More replies can now be attempted, so reply rates and
costs cannot be compared directly with the frozen ordinary pilot or diagnostic.
Those sparse runs did not test this mechanism and establish no vocabulary or
survival benefit.
