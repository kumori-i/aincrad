# Neutral paid hand motion

The two ordinary Ozark actors can try one target-free hand lift during optional
exploration. It is a supplied motor affordance, not a word, request, greeting,
clarification convention, or learned action policy. It requires no partner and
elicits no automatic reply. Its fixed curiosity score of 2.5 initially
competes with other optional choices only after bodily care, reference
interactions, and the existing experimental choice hook. The score falls as
that actor attempts the motion; no outcome or predictor score increases it.
An actor waits at least 120 simulation seconds after a fresh start or reopen
and between attempted admissions. The rate budget is spent when admission is
attempted, including an interruption. These choices and delays are authored
exploration scaffolding, not discovered communicative meaning.

The action has no object ID or pointing permit. It requires 1.8 seconds of
actual human effort funding at effort 0.25, with a six-second work deadline
under current funding rules. At the current human walking cost this adds
0.48 kcal of effort expenditure when fully paid, alongside ongoing basal
metabolism. Partial paid work still costs energy and does not count as a
completed action. An urgent bodily state, interrupted identity, position
drift, collapse, or disposal cancels the action. Its paid pose then disappears.
It can displace time that might have been used for survival or object study.

Rendering and actor-local sight read the same numeric segment after paid
progress. A visible partner receives only relative pose geometry and progress,
never the motor action name or an inferred intention. The actor can retain its
own action state and paid geometry in session-only gesture memory. Its
session-only predictor freezes these own-context features when forecasting
future personally visible pose or absence, and scores a forecast before an
update. The predictor cannot select the motion or supply a character-message
reward. The object-indication parser still reads only paid object indications;
the neutral lift cannot resolve an object or train either character model.

Observer-only bounded counters distinguish eligible optional decisions,
attempted admissions, and actual actor-local samples of a visible partner
motion. They are decision/sample counts, not time duration or learned meaning.
The counters and gesture/predictor sessions do not enter saves. In an ordinary
world the choice may be rare, and a peer may be absent or occluded when it
occurs. No vocabulary, reciprocal repair, survival benefit, or human-like
communication is established by the availability of this motor action.
