## Predeclared readout

For each actor and each **travel, acquisition and sample** stage, report
selected requests, normalized requests, physical admissions, first certified
paid work, certified paid seconds, completed and failed actions, interruptions,
instant completions, immediate rejections and unavailable witnesses. A
selected request is a decision, not an admission. A first-paid count is one
action, not a tick; certified paid seconds are duration. Reach categories
describe the actor's remembered own source position or held material at the
decision. They do not prove physical arrival. Report the fixed physical
rejection/interruption codes and run-end reasons, including proof retirement,
without exporting target IDs, routes, private features or action authority.
Run ends count intentions and cannot be equated with physical completions;
one may occur before any selected action. Pending loss, identity changes and
session resets censor later outcome rather than fabricate an interruption or
success. Explicit omitted-key and saturation fields qualify every inference.

Reconcile unsaturated stage counters within each continuous actor session.
Segment at identity, epoch or observer-session resets. A pending action seen
in one sparse projection can settle before the next; record that uncertainty.
Do not infer that the same action appears in two projections, or convert a
lost receipt into a completion. Report controller food-run decision counters
separately from this action witness, with their respective units and bounds.

Report ingested, absorbed, discarded, pending digestive and metabolized kcal
as distinct quantities, alongside available and stored energy, health,
satiety, starvation hours, meals, drinks, collapses and rescues. Aggregate
pending kcal privately from stomach portions; no portion row or food identity
is exported. Reconcile intake against absorbed, discarded and pending kcal
within numeric tolerance. Do not claim reserve change equals that ledger if
rescue or another physical transfer adds energy. A bodily `independentMeals`
event is delayed feedback, not a full meal. For the character channel retain
separate opportunity, paid inspection/reply, unique demonstration, replay,
optimizer and sender-update denominators. Censors are not wrong choices.

Primary questions: Does a learned food run get selected, admitted and reach
its personally remembered resource? Does physical travel complete, and does
acquisition or sampling begin and finish with paid work? At which fixed
stage/reason do observed runs end? Does personally acquired intake cover any
meaningful fraction of expenditure after accounting for digestion? Sparse or
zero events mean insufficient evidence, not policy failure. Preserve actual
survival interruptions and do not infer a particular cause from a generic
reason. This same-seed, source-changed follow-up is **descriptive**, not an
independent replication, causal comparison, proof of sustainable survival or
evidence of shared word meanings. Controlled native fixtures check freshness,
privacy and projection shape; they do not establish a full learned food-run
