# Peer teaching and small hints

Aster and Mira can share an exact-source procedure during an actual nearby conversation. The speaker must have retained her own acquisition, sample and later bodily feedback. The listener can try the taught operations immediately without first rediscovering the sequence or proving the food benefit herself.

## What is shared

The first slice shares `pull → sample` and `grasp → sample` procedures for an exact source the listener already recognizes. This includes processing a known carcass. It does not automatically teach a general hunting strategy, a species-wide recipe, safe edibility, practiced handling, or the other character's memories.

Nearby journal entries are labelled “taught procedure”. The listener's knowledge identifies the speaker, time since hearing, operations and whether the advice remains untried, is awaiting her own feedback, has been supported or contradicted by her own experience, or could not be attempted.

Speech is not food or practice. Each physical step still pays its normal handling time and bodily cost, requires local reach and free hands, and uses finite material. Full hands or a vanished source mean the attempt was unavailable, not that the speaker's reported nutritional experience was false. Genuine adverse feedback can contradict the advice.

Advice is admitted for one bounded attempt within 900 unpaused play seconds after receipt, one quarter of the 3600-second game day. Old testimony stays visible. Urgent bodily care and safety continue to take priority. No food changes hands in this feature.

## Giving a small hint

Use an object ID from the selected character's observed-object list, or an unambiguous visible description she already recognizes, and an operation to try. For example:

- `try catch object:123`
- `strike object:456`
- `pull object:789, then sample it`

The `kill` verb proposes a strike attempt. It does not guarantee a kill, acquisition or meal. `catch` is available only where the physical action exists and still requires the character's own fresh sighting. Neither word grants a result or food knowledge.

A vague message such as “you can kill the animals” remains a report when no exact observed target can be identified. The character says that she needs a specific target instead of consulting a hidden list of animals. Advice that cannot become an executable trial is also labelled in the knowledge view.

## Persistence boundary

The current save retains attributed advice separately from personal proof. Progress is reconstructed only from the recipient's own matching action and feedback records. Interrupted or missing acquisition links disable an unfinished taught attempt rather than replaying it. Loading advice never manufactures material, ownership, proficiency or a successful meal.
