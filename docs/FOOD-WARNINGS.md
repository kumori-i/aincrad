# Adverse-material warnings

An agent with a retained, validated personal sample and later adverse condition
can warn an awake, nearby, visible peer. The ordinary dialogue path prioritizes
this warning over positive procedure teaching and food-sharing chatter, but not
urgent bodily care. Delivery is synchronous; proposing speech is not delivery.
The speaker says it sampled the exact material and later felt discomfort, with
explicit uncertainty about cause and other exposures.

The listener stores a separate **unverified attributed report**. A report whose
exact material is now held and personally identified can defer autonomous
sampling for 900 simulation seconds after receipt. This gate covers ordinary,
learned, taught and observational-inquiry sample selection. It is not a physical
immunity rule or a ban on explicitly commanded physical actions. The listener
receives no personal effects, practice, technique, calories or item from speech.

Dropping and regaining the same physical material does not change its identity.
An optional original-source reference comes only from a unique, retained,
validated personal pull receipt; a later grasp is not an invented new origin.
Source information does **not** extend this warning to other portions from that
source, visually similar materials or a species. Missing lineage stays unknown.
Existing positive and negative procedure attribution/execution rules are unchanged.

## Bounded persistence

Optional schema-12 `warning` metadata on the delivered peer message and ordinary
report carries an allowlisted snapshot of the sender's sample, adverse
recognition and optional pull closure. These are testimony-validation records,
never recipient firsthand ledgers. Report, heard message, sender identity,
material, receipt time and canonical wording must agree. Unknown fields,
inconsistent IDs/times, duplicate crosslinks and malformed proof are rejected by
the current save validator. This is save consistency, not cryptographic
protection against a wholly fabricated, mutually consistent save.

Snapshots are bounded by the existing heard/report limits and carry no private
location, species, body reserves or full sender history. Sender compaction does
not erase a delivered report. Heard FIFO eviction retires its matching optional
warning authority while preserving ordinary prose. Reload restores only already
received caution, without replay, refreshed expiry or completion of interrupted
physical work. Expiry is not positive safety evidence or an instruction to retry.

## Independent personal source caution

A separate own-source guard can defer sampling another held portion only when
the actor's own retained extraction, sample and adverse-condition receipts form
an exact valid chain. It does not use donor knowledge or hidden ecology ancestry.
Four recent own extraction origins, plus origins needed by held materials, are
retained under bounded proof rules. Missing or ambiguous evidence means the
source is unknown. This personal guard does not broaden a received peer warning:
peer testimony still concerns only its exact material.

Both guards coexist with direct personal adverse evidence at the central
sampling eligibility boundary. Source caution, warning expiry and absent
feedback are never general food-safety or species classifications.

## Verification and limits

Controlled tests use real paid samples, physical spoilage, delayed recognition
and actual speech delivery. The warning comparison branches identical complete
state and RNG, removing only the recipient's warning report in the control arm.
The control must actually select the held sample; otherwise the observation is
not evidence of warning-driven avoidance. Separate range, sight and sleeping
controls test failed delivery. No physical gift or personal proof follows speech.

An earlier combined-runtime observation selected inspection with the warning
versus sampling without its authority, 3.7 simulation seconds after receipt.
This was a controlled opportunity, not spontaneous travel or a free-roaming
missed encounter. The control choice was not physically administered, so the
comparison does not establish a health benefit. Source identities, historical
failed/inconclusive opportunities and the current release rerun status are
recorded in [verification](food-system/VERIFICATION.md).

`scripts/verify-food-warning-desktop.mjs --prepare-only` prepares an isolated
three-process UI/save/reopen runner. Preparation and Node fixture tests are not
native execution. Native screenshots and actual close/reopen remain **not run**
until the corresponding Linux/macOS CI verification completes on the release
source; no display or sandbox restriction may be bypassed.

The ordinary sample remains 30 g. Earlier benefit can precede recognized harm,
and substantial exposure may occur before the adverse guard activates. Absence
of recognized discomfort is not proof of safety. A smaller proposed taste would
need separate detection and delayed-learning validation; no safe dose, universal
waiting period, reliable warning timeliness or self-sustaining survival is claimed.
