# Reusable prediction: bounded design draft, not implementation approval

Date: 2026-10-16. Review revision: 1. Status: proposed architecture and falsification gates only; not a frozen preregistration.
Authorized next scope, effective only after final hash review: the bounded model/sensor implementation and immutable fixtures described here, plus the specified copied-world 288-hour aged-material/spoil intervention only. No autonomous episodes, cohort, publication, main/default-controller promotion, or changes to live inhabitants/saves are authorized. This revision task itself performs no runtime edits or assays. PR #9 and the failed original interactive gate remain unchanged.

## Recommendation in one paragraph

Use small, partially pooled **factorized tables for observed physical outcomes and exposure outcomes**, with an exact safety/provenance ledger alongside them. Do not merely replace the current exact Q key with operation plus appearance: that would share the old-meal confound as efficiently as useful experience. Learn what an operation physically changed, separately from what a newly created exposure later felt like, and value those predictions using current sensations. First approve only model/sensor fixtures and immutable matched choice probes. Establish that one earned delayed outcome is reused on the same material across irrelevant context changes, while an old exposure cannot create an unrelated action's benefit. Keep generic sequence composition as an explicitly separate next increment. This is progress toward learned affordances, not a new two-step food technique.

## 1. What the evidence establishes

The existing ledger/model replay exactly matches the three frozen runs. Online ultimately has 98 food-positive Q rows but selects zero food-positive forecasts; this is not a missing-update demonstration. There is no row/dictionary capacity saturation. Of 120 distinct online selected exact keys, only 29 operation/complete-appearance groups remain when other distinctions are descriptively projected away. That is evidence of possible sharing, not validation of that projection.

The nearest-prior analysis strengthens the joint-fragmentation diagnosis: 92/100/88 unknown selections have a previously closed operation/appearance match; only 11/14/9 differ in one field. Online material 868 at sample submissions 136 and 153 has the same body, appearance, environment, held relation/count and observed object count; focus, aggregate work band, last-two history and exposure summary differ. The material identifier identifies the same object/stack, not the same swallowed bite. Positive F/W feedback intervenes, but the later key remains unknown.

The existing return predictor describes whole trajectories. It does not establish action causality. No inference about unseen candidate identities, general survival, optimal horizons, or another policy's outcome follows from the compact traces.

## 2. Compare only three options

1. **Coarsened exact Q table:** cheapest code change; still learns pending old-meal associations and collapses important state. Reject as the next hypothesis.
2. **Partially pooled outcome tables:** recommended. Counts, monitored exposure time, exact source bindings and pooling contributions are inspectable. Same appearance can share evidence without claiming interchangeable identities. No dense optimizer or network is needed.
3. **Sparse linear multi-head predictor:** reserve if the table gate exposes excessive residual fragmentation. Operation-by-feature interactions can avoid Cartesian keys, but regularization and uncertainty become another confounded design choice. A neural/recurrent model is not justified by these traces.

Do not change representation, exploration, rewards, physiological timing and planning depth together. The first increment is a prediction/use test, not a full autonomous-policy comparison.

## 3. Three distinct records and their allowed information

### A. Exact evidence and safety ledger: preserve the existing contract

Keep exact request/receipt/action/material/ingestion/event identities, chronological ownership, channel bitmasks, safety exclusions and directed one-hop production bindings. Release/regrasp replaces a former acquisition association; a sibling never inherits another material's safety. Reopen, proof expiry and censored history must retain the current fail-closed behavior.

IDs may select the exact binding and tell the decision adapter whether this material has firsthand evidence. They are never numerical features, categorical predictor keys, species labels, or a mechanism for sharing safety. An opaque-ID bijection preserving relations must preserve predictions and corresponding choices.

### B. Reusable stable outcome model

Separate these heads; do not concatenate all their inputs into one key:

- **Physical effect head:** operation and observed material appearance; held/reach/route relation; observed capacity/free-slot band; observed target-count band; personally completed work on this target for the relevant operation; most recent *observed physical state change* if any. Predict categorical observable effects, duration and felt work.
- **New-exposure head:** observed appearance at swallowing, qualitative sensations at swallowing, exposure age, and actually reported channel status. Predict the timing of additional F/W/H reports from that exposure. It does not take current focus, unrelated operation history, or other materials' complete exposure vectors as a material property.
- **Uncertainty/support metadata:** independent completed receipts, independent ingestions, monitored time, censored records, parent/child pooling weights and exact-versus-transferred support. Repeated training passes never increase experience counts.

Observable physical effects are generic: unchanged/refused/interrupted, a target becoming held, a held target becoming released, a distinct observed output appearing, an exposure actually being created, and an observed route completing. Actual result codes are translated only when their public receipt proves the corresponding relation. Unknown is a separate outcome. No hand-authored effect probability, successful operation order, food class or species mapping is installed.

Do not treat all past work as nuisance. Cumulative personally observed work on a still-bound target can matter for slow manipulation. It belongs in the relevant physical-effect head, not in every biological prediction key. Do not infer hidden progress from it. A genuine observed transformation invalidates the old appearance/state association; a mere change in focus or last-two-history serialization does not.

### C. Current state/value projection

Retain all seven public body-sensation categories, thermal/shelter sensations, held/reach relations, capacity, current eligibility, own sampled/pending status, directed output bindings and independently represented pending exposures. Exact IDs resolve these relations; the resulting public relation values are features.

Current hunger/thirst change value, not the stored identity of an operation's past outcome. Keep the current scalarization for this increment: F weights .5/1/2, W weights .5/1/2, H weight -4, time and felt-work weights -.02. Fullness, exact harm aversion, pending-sample caution, reach, capacity and all emergency care remain the existing action masks. Focus remains only the existing tie scaffold.

A censoring flag widens uncertainty or disables an unproved relation. It does not create a new copy of every learned outcome row.

## 4. Concrete proposed table/update specification

These numerical values are proposed engineering constants, not fitted conclusions. Freeze them before collecting new observations. They may be rejected during design review; do not tune them against the reserved probes after execution.

### 4.1 Physical outcome rows

A row stores separate effect-observation and closed-cost-interval counts, completion-status counts, observed relation-delta indicator counts with their own observed denominators, completed action-duration sum/sum-of-squares, discounted T/E target sums/sum-of-squares, and last-admitted receipt/interval sequences. One receipt can both create a distinct output and make it held: increment its experience count once and each proved indicator once. Do not assume those indicators are independent or call their marginals a joint transition distribution. Effect and completion-duration updates occur once at authoritative completion. T/E updates occur once when the next actual submission closes the interval. An open/censored interval is not a zero-cost example; an unobserved effect is not an observed no-effect count. No update increments both experience counts as two physical experiences.

Use this explicit hierarchy for effect/duration/work prediction:

- parent: operation × relation;
- material child: operation × complete observed appearance × relation;
- work child: the material child × relevant-operation work band.

Use the existing work bands initially. Capacity and observed count are **separate conditional heads**, not new dimensions of this whole hierarchy: operation × relation × free-slot band, and operation × observed-count band. They predict their corresponding capacity/count effects and refusal risks. A root candidate's true existing eligibility always outranks a statistical prediction. If a required condition is unobserved, expose unknown rather than extrapolating an impossible success.

For each effect/status indicator child with n observed labels, use `(child count + 4 × parent mean)/(n + 4)`; for completion duration and closed-interval T/E, use the same four-observation shrinkage with each head's own observed denominator. A parent without observations is unknown, not a fabricated uniform successful effect. Report parent and child support separately; a parent containing the child observations is not an independent extra experience. The first increment scores only effects supported by actual completed receipts; it does not assume conditional heads are a calibrated joint distribution.

For increment A, define `p_new(a)` as the new-exposure indicator mean from the deepest supported work/material/operation-relation row. The actual current mask must already admit a; its observed capacity/count eligibility is a supplied constraint, not an estimated probability. Do not multiply the separate capacity/count marginals into p_new or invent their joint distribution. Their prediction errors remain diagnostics and falsifiers. In particular, count/capacity interactions not represented by the actual mask or primary head are an explicit limitation, not silently corrected by a heuristic. The conditional heads preserve capacity/count distinctions without requiring every body/history/exposure combination to recur. The simplification may miss interactions; the causal-state falsifiers below are intended to reject it when that matters.

### 4.2 Exposure outcome rows

Use the existing four age thresholds, with an explicit event-endpoint convention for the rate statistics: (0,45], (45,180], (180,600], (600,infinity) seconds. Time integrals use the corresponding adjacent intervals; endpoints have zero duration. An event at exactly 45/180/600 belongs to the positive-risk interval ending there, not the just-entered zero-duration bin. This statistical bin choice never changes the authoritative event timestamp or reward owner. A zero-age event violates the current delayed-feedback contract: preserve its real ledger/safety handling, mark this rate predictor unsupported and stop progression, rather than divide by zero or invent elapsed risk. Any E>0 with R<=0 is a model-integrity failure, not a reason to add epsilon seconds. Forecasts in the first probe are bounded to **600 future seconds**; the final bin remains monitored and explicitly tail-uncertain. Neither crossing a bin nor reaching the probe horizon certifies safety.

For each channel c and age bin b, maintain actual event count E and observed at-risk time R. A channel is at risk from a proved swallow until its first report, censoring, or loss of its retained proof. Food and water reports do not end the harm risk process. A later H report remains independent of earlier F/W. A single combined message can close several distinct channel processes once each.

Rate-table hierarchy:

- parent: channel × age bin;
- material child: complete observed appearance × channel × age bin;
- digestion child: material child × qualitative digestion sensation at swallowing (unknown/empty/settled/full/overfull). This is the deepest active leaf, pooled toward its material parent by the same rule. Hunger/thirst and all other public sensations remain available in the current state/value projection; store their swallowing snapshots for interaction-error diagnostics, without crossing them into this rate key. If omitted sensation interactions materially violate the prediction gate, reject this simplification instead of claiming the stable material model is sufficient.

At each observed child level use a piecewise constant empirical rate `(E_child + 30 × rate_parent)/(R_child + 30)`, with time in seconds. A parent with no observed at-risk time is unknown; its numerical point estimate may be zero, but never its uncertainty. The 30 seconds is a fixed pooling strength, not a biological digestion constant. At the parent use E/R when R>0. Channel rates yield per-exposure forecast mass `S_c(t) × rate_c(t) × dt`, with a **separate** survival term `S_c(t)=exp(-integral_0^t rate_c(u)du)` for each channel, discounted with the existing 300-second half-life. For a still-unreported channel at known age a, condition on surviving to a by integrating from a forward. Stop only that channel after its first event; an F event does not reset or terminate S_H or S_W. The marginal channel models allow both benefit and later harm for one exposure.

This is a small piecewise event-rate predictor, not a claimed calibrated biological model. Log unsupported age bins and pooling dependence. A rate of zero with no observed events is not proof of no hazard or no eventual food. A censored exposure contributes only the time genuinely observed; it is never a completed zero-outcome trial. A feedback event updates the relevant exposure statistics immediately after chronological ledger admission, not at a later action submission.

At-risk increments and event increments are associated with the exposure's stored public snapshot. The currently performed inspect/wait/strike does not receive that exposure's label. Supervision can use a proved ingestion link without relocating the reward event in the ledger.

**Proposed supervised-label admission adapter:** snapshot the canonical matched ingestion's channel bits/proofs and both global/ingestion `creditBlocked` flags immediately before the single existing `recordTemporalFeedback` call. After it returns, admit only newly added channel bits whose new `channelProof[c]` has that exact authoritative event source sequence/time and whose retained successful swallow receipt still proves object/action/ingestion identity. Both global and ingestion creditBlocked must be false before and after. Use the post-call canonical proof delta, not the function's boolean: current `src/temporal-ledger.js` lines 61–64 can install a valid new proof and return false because no open known reward owner exists. Such an unowned but valid observation may supervise this outcome model once; it adds no reward or owner and never increments ledger totals. An ingestion/global credit block, lost swallow proof, invalid source/time, future event, or duplicate/no-new-bit result supplies no new supervised label. Censor affected exposure prediction if continuous admissible observation is lost; do not reinterpret missing admissible feedback as observed no harm.

Persist a separate per-ingestion/channel supervision-admitted mask/proof reference and risk watermark alongside the bounded exposure projection. A duplicate callback, save validation, replay, or reopen cannot train it again. Do not call the ledger again to obtain a convenient true return, re-create an evicted proof, or reset its source cursor. Restored validated proofs restore already-admitted state but trigger no training. Unknown-context reward ownership and valid biological supervision are reported separately. This adapter is a proposed consumer contract, not a change to ledger reward admission.

Proposed exact accrual contract: each proved exposure stores three `riskThroughAt` timestamps initialized to its actual swallow time, plus reported/censored bits and its frozen public swallowing snapshot. At a callback, determine the canonical proof delta through the admission adapter, then flush every still-open admissibly monitored channel from its watermark to that authoritative event time, splitting the elapsed seconds at age-bin boundaries; then increment only newly admitted supervised event channel(s), mark those channels closed, and retain the other channels. The statistics treat the risk interval as ending immediately before the event, while retaining the event's true timestamp. A benign duplicate adds no event and no duplicated risk; an invalid/blocked continuity path follows the censoring rule instead of inventing observation time. At an ordinary sensor/decision boundary, flush still-open channels to the current shared simulation clock. Before proof eviction, flush through the last continuously monitored clock and censor the exposure. Every flush advances its watermark atomically; a second flush at the same clock adds zero time. A paused clock adds nothing, and a reopen gap with unproved continuity is censored rather than credited as observed survival. Biological callbacks during sleep use the same ordering. No additional physiology tick, free observation or per-candidate tick work occurs. The native sensor supplies continuous event capture; only bounded bookkeeping is batched. Independent review must verify this ordering against the actual emission/eviction/restore APIs before implementation approval.

### 4.3 Uncertainty is explicit, with a deliberately conservative use rule

For each F/W/H forecast report point estimate, independent ingestions, event count, monitored-time/age coverage, pooling weight, and channel-specific exact-material support. Report an epistemic range of [0,1] for a new material's channel outcome over the horizon; do not advertise this as a calibrated confidence interval. Sparse firsthand experience also remains uncertain. This first increment does not attempt statistical safety certification.

A new lookalike may receive a transferred *prediction*. Positive support is **channel-specific and state-bound**: F contributes positive value only after a retained valid binding proves F for this exact material in the still-applicable observed material appearance/state; W requires its own matching W report. The support record binds channel, exact material, observed appearance and the last proved observed-material-transformation generation at the earned swallow. For increment A, transformation generation changes only on an actually observed change in the declared six-field material-appearance signature (`shape`, `color`, `texture`, `motion`, `size`, `smell`); count depletion, a successful sample itself, held/released/regrasped relation, position, focus and history never increment it, and no additional state or transformation signals are admitted. Exact-material firsthand support may survive release/regrasp only with proven identity and a currently observed applicable six-field appearance/state; production/source linkage still follows the existing release/regrasp invalidation. An unobserved interval cannot establish absence of transformation: if the continuity/current-state contract cannot prove applicability, support is unknown rather than restored optimistically. Quantity reduction is not a transformation. A genuine observed material transformation or incompatible new appearance invalidates positive use of the old support; a focus/history serialization change does not. Current need sensations remain separate valuation/model conditions, not a replacement opaque support key. If a supposedly matching material-state relation is unproved, support is unknown. W-only experience does not unlock transferred F, and F does not unlock W. H information is never blocked merely because exact H has not happened: pooled harm prediction/uncertainty remains available conservatively, and exact H exclusions stay authoritative. It remains in the unchanged candidate set and can still be sampled by the existing group-uniform exploration. This is a forecast-use gate, not an edibility mask or exclusion of an eligible primitive. Physical nonbiological effects can reuse their own learned support independently.

For ranking, an unsupported positive-channel point estimate is retained in diagnostics but contributes zero to U; this is withheld positive evidence, not a trained zero biological outcome. Firsthand F/W does not mean safe. Exact harm exclusions stay authoritative; any observed H cannot be canceled by positive channels. For transferred H use at least the applicable parent harm prediction rather than shrinking a material with no harm experience below it. Mixed good/bad outcomes in a sensory group must widen the reported uncertainty; never count many portions from one item as independent evidence about many identities.

This conservative gate intentionally limits initial positive transfer. It is specific to increment A, not the final generalization policy: newly produced acquisition outputs will otherwise have no firsthand biological support and cannot acquire useful multi-step value under this rule. A generic acquisition extension must explicitly revisit how uncertain value propagates to those new outputs; merely attaching a planner to this gate would not solve acquisition. It is falsifiable and may leave new-lookalike exploitation poor. Relaxing it would be a new experiment requiring a justified calibration/safety criterion; it is not an automatic follow-up.

## 5. Pending baseline versus an action's additional consequence

At a decision, let P be the set of already-proved unresolved exposures. Compute a baseline B_c(P,H) for their remaining reports over the **same H=600-second simulation-elapsed horizon for every candidate, on the native shared clock**, using current known channel statuses and ages. B is logged and can inform need-conditioned state interpretation; it is never attached as an extra reward to an unrelated action.

Compute `D_c(a,H) = p_new(a) * exp(-beta*d_a) * G_c(A, digestion_at_swallow, max(0,H-d_a))`, where p_new is defined in 4.1, d_a is the supported physical-head expected time to completion, and G is the discounted rate integral of a newly created exposure from age zero. For increment A the next swallow sensation is approximated by the current public digestion sensation; do not query future hidden body state. Flag that approximation and reject the model if the sensation-interaction gate exposes material error. A root action whose learned head has no supported new-exposure consequence has unknown D, not an invented zero-success observation; it cannot gain positive exploitation support from that head. An observed non-exposure action has D=0. The first increment uses this one-step new-exposure term and expected discounted time/work costs:

`U_new(a) = wF*D_F(a) + wW*D_W(a) - 4*D_H(a) - .02*T(a) - .02*E(a)`.

Cost units are fixed to the existing ledger: T is the discounted integral of elapsed seconds/60 from actual submission through the next actual submission, including true scheduler waiting; E is the discounted integral of the existing ordinal exertion rate (light 1/3, moderate 2/3, strenuous 1) over actually worked seconds/60. Both use beta=ln(2)/300 from that interval's start. The learned targets are the ledger's own closed-interval T/E channels with no successor/bootstrap component. No raw seconds enter the -.02 coefficients, no second discount is applied, and canceled/unfinished work is not fabricated as zero. The separate completion-duration head d_a, in seconds, is used only to offset the new exposure forecast.

`V_pending(s) = wF*B_F + wW*B_W - 4*B_H` is a separate state forecast. Adding the identical V_pending to all candidate displays is allowed but must not change ranking. Wait can have a positive total future-state forecast while its **new-exposure biological term stays zero**. Unequal candidate durations must not earn unequal shares of the old meal merely because one interval spans its report.

For the bounded increment, no learned action effect on an old exposure's digestion/hazard is assumed. If the real mechanism has such effects, this is a model limitation requiring separate controlled evidence. Genuine elapsed costs still count. Sensations and existing pending/fullness masks still constrain repeated sampling.

Every biological reward remains exactly once, at its original biological time. Learning D from that event is supervised prediction, not a second reward at sample or acquisition. B changes as known events arrive; no new ledger event is issued when a forecast moves between action and state descriptions. Old whole-trajectory Q may remain an owner diagnostic but must not leak back into the proposed D score.

### Selector support and matched frozen control

Replace the old exact-key known flag with an explicitly narrower `scorable` flag: the candidate has at least one genuine observed parent operation/relation effect and a closed interval supplying its cost heads; relevant bindings are valid. A material child may use that parent by the declared pooling rule. An unknown operation/cost head is not an optimistic synthetic zero exploitation winner. Unsupported positive F/W is withheld as above; negative H and actual masks remain applicable. A candidate can therefore be scorable for physical costs while having no positive biological support. This is not a safe/edible label, and it does not prohibit the primitive.

Preserve the existing selector rule: epsilon=.2 uses uniform primitive-group/within-group exploration over the unchanged eligible set; otherwise maximize U among scorable candidates; if none are scorable, use the same exploration fallback. Preserve focus ties and all four committed draws. A new lookalike might win on a genuinely supported nonbiological cost comparison, but cannot win because transferred F/W was counted as earned support.

Increment A's matched frozen control starts from the **same pre-feedback physical-model checkpoint** after the actual sample receipt and its cost interval have closed. Freeze all prediction parameters, including rate/cost/effect estimates, there. Continue actual exact ledger/safety/binding and channel-support bookkeeping identically in both copies. Both therefore acquire the same channel-specific evidence-use flags when the real report arrives; only the online predictor changes numerical estimates. Query the same current post-feedback state/candidate snapshot, weights and forced selector draws against both parameter copies. This prevents a changing eligibility/support registry from masquerading as numerical learning. The model-update statistics and the factual support registry must be serialized separately or demonstrably separated within a row. Cold frozen-zero autonomy is a different control and is not specified or authorized in this increment.

## 6. Generic sequence learning: clear route, separate approval

Increment A does **not** claim that a direct new-exposure predictor discovers acquisition sequences or beneficial capacity-management plans. It establishes reusable outcome semantics and prediction use first.

Existing authoritative receipts supply evidence of generic operations and observed output/binding changes. Increment A learns only marginal outcome heads; they do **not** form a composable successor-state graph. A later joint-transition model would need paired public pre-/post-state snapshots, effect correlations, observed eligibility and corresponding uncertainty before its learned edges could support `cost + expected successor value` evaluation under current need. An operation that newly holds a useful object can gain value because its empirically observed successor has a supported action consequence, regardless of which primitive produced it. Another operation could expose an output, move it, free a slot, or interrupt a costly process. No code path says “pull then sample,” and no operation-name sequence receives a fixed bonus.

Before that extension, require a separate preregistration of composition depth, partial-observability handling, joint-transition uncertainty, loop handling, and budget. A useful initial depth might be 2–3, but it is not approved here. No imagined world calls, hidden source inspection, seeded graph edges or automatic model-generated action scripts. Only actual root actions use the unchanged authoritative mask; hypothetical successors must use retained observed relation/eligibility evidence and cannot query hidden future world state. Unknown edges stay unknown.

A later useful-release test must prove capacity benefit through a learned transition to an observed available opportunity, rather than rewarding release itself. Initial A can establish non-compulsion to continue and correct release effects, not that multi-step capacity planning works.

### Gate 1: earned same-material reuse, before/after and frozen controls

Prepare 16 matched query variants from one eligible held-material opportunity: nuisance focus/history serialization and unrelated exposure-age/channel summaries may vary, but causal held/sample/capacity/sensation facts remain fixed. Do not vary a genuine observed material transformation. Read all variants from one immutable snapshot; no extra physical action is executed to create queries. These 16 queries are one earned-update sensitivity test, not 16 independent learning examples or 16 accuracy outcomes. They must not contribute repeated weight to a prediction-quality average.

For the cold beneficial fixture, require one real proved F report before exposure age 180 seconds, no prior F observations, one successful exposure-creating action with supported duration <=30 seconds, no conflicting observed transformation, and a genuinely remaining eligible portion of the same exact material. These are explicit assay conditions, not a claim about every natural trajectory. If they are absent, mark this assay unavailable. No sibling, newly produced lookalike, replenishment or reinserted object may substitute for the remaining portion. Add explicit exhausted-item and final-small-portion controls: actual removal/exhaustion must remove its sample opportunity, and a final smaller portion may legitimately fail to produce a thresholded F report; neither is a reason to fabricate support or require another bite. Under these conditions the supported new-exposure F forecast must increase by >=0.05 discounted event units versus the identical pre-feedback model in all 16 variants. The nuisance variants must agree within 1e-9. Under a preset exploitation draw and an available comparison action, the changed model must change at least one previously nonpreferred eligible choice to the experienced material, with >=0.05 utility margin; costs/need weights/masks/RNG are held fixed. A frozen-parameter control must show zero forecast and rank change. Report H uncertainty and any legitimate pending mask blocking that particular choice; never bypass the mask to pass.

Why 0.05 is plausible rather than a tuned promise: in the minimal one-exposure/no-other-history mathematics fixture, the first event gives rate 1/r in its age bin at every pooled level, because parent and child contain the same one observation; r is the observed at-risk time in that bin. Its full-bin event mass is at least 1-exp(-1). With an event before 180 seconds, discount through that bin is at least exp(-beta*180); a <=30-second action adds at least exp(-beta*30). Thus the model-generated discounted point forecast is at least about 0.39 when p_new=1. This is a check of the proposed equations under stated assumptions, not evidence of accurate biology or safety. The 0.05 change/margin gates are deliberately below that algebraic signal. More complex histories may not satisfy that bound and need separately declared probes, not threshold tuning. If no valid opportunity with both choices exists, the gate is unavailable, not passed. Include the actual 136->153 feature difference as a regression illustration, but do not pretend the old compact trace supplies an entire unrecorded choice set.

- Four proposed handling variants of that same assay are ordinary chronological capture, reopen before H, an unrelated ongoing operation at H, and competing pending exposures with repeated message presentation. They are four bookkeeping controls, not four independent biological discoveries. In each, actual engine H must follow its own exposure's earlier F or W. Every H is retained once; earlier positive channels never close harm monitoring or label the material safe. Exact masking blocks a forbidden next sample. The correctly bound H forecast must increase, and an unrelated action's D_H remains zero.
- A stop or lost proof before H does not become a zero-harm terminal. A benefit-only observation does not pass the delayed-harm fixture. No adversarial toxicity trials are authorized by this draft.
- At least four fixtures contrast continuing a demonstrated costly/nonproductive manipulation with an eligible release or departure. The physical head predicts the observed release/capacity effect correctly; all these actions remain in the root choice set. On preset exploitation probes, release/departure must not rank below continued work because of transferred old-meal F/W. Match costs explicitly; do not require release to beat a truly cheaper wait without a demonstrated capacity benefit.
- A full “free a slot, acquire, then benefit” choice gate is deferred with generic sequence composition. Do not relabel mere release eligibility as learned useful-release planning.

### Gate 5: prediction quality and negative-transfer report

Compare factorized and matched frozen-factorized predictors on the same labels. The old exact Q predicts discounted whole-trajectory returns, which may exceed one; it is **not** a probability predictor and must never be fed to Brier or exposure log-loss comparisons. Retain it only for its native return/ranking diagnostics. A future exact-feature outcome-model control would need the identical outcome labels/horizons and its own preregistration; it is not added here.

For physical effects, issue each prediction before the next actual action receipt, then score resolved completion/status indicators using Brier score. Report coverage/unknown cases separately; do not silently drop them from the denominator. For biological quality, after the training feedback take at least one **later, genuinely new ingestion** of the still-available exact material through an actual authorized action. Before that ingestion's feedback, freeze both models' F/W/H probabilities of a report within the next 600 simulation seconds. Use undiscounted integrated channel probabilities for Brier score, not discounted D values or whole-trajectory Q. Score only after complete public follow-up for the corresponding horizon; a lost proof or interrupted observation is censored and has no fabricated zero label. A channel report during the horizon is a factual positive; no report under continuous complete follow-up is a zero *within that horizon*, never a lifetime safety conclusion. Record discounted prediction error separately when its target is fully observed.

The finite material may be exhausted or pending/fullness masks may prevent the later ingestion. Then quality evaluation is unavailable: do not replenish the item, relax masks, or score its training event as a held-out prediction. All 16 nuisance queries for one event still count as one sensitivity test, and repeated portions from one material are explicitly clustered by identity. One later event establishes only a narrow engineering check, not calibrated population-level accuracy.

Report quality separately for firsthand repeat, new identity/same appearance, distinct appearance, conflicting outcomes and delayed tail whenever an authorized assay supplies those observations. An absent category is untested, not passed. Proposed admission criterion for the declared later-ingestion probe: fully observed F/W/H Brier mean improves >=20% relative to the matched frozen-factorized predictor, and resolved physical outcome Brier in the declared causal-state/conflict controls degrades by no more than 0.02 absolute. Report actual independent materials/ingestions and uncertainty; these proposed thresholds are rejection criteria, not guarantees or statistical significance. The invariant and safety gates permit zero violations. If the frozen denominator is zero, required categories lack observations, or follow-up is censored, mark inconclusive and stop; do not tune or extend the horizon opportunistically.

An advantage at these gates is evidence of reusable prediction and bounded forecast use. It is not evidence of autonomous acquisition, net nutrition, safe transfer, or survival. A later autonomous comparison must be separately authorized with its own frozen starts/horizon and unchanged care/exploration controls.

## 8. Review decisions still open, diagnostics and stopping condition

This is not yet a frozen preregistration. Independent design review must accept or reject: (1) the empirical rate approximation and zero-event point estimates with explicit unknown risk; (2) using only current eligibility plus the primary physical head for p_new while separate capacity/count heads remain diagnostic; (3) the stated event/accrual ordering and continuous-observation assumption; (4) compliance with the narrowly authorized copied-world aged-material assay scope; and (5) the proposed numerical error/resource thresholds. These choices are small enough to review without implementing a planner. Rejecting one is a design outcome, not permission to silently substitute another model.

### Required diagnostic output and stopping condition

For every candidate in a future authorized probe, record full public feature groups, root mask/eligibility, physical head predictions, B and D separately, need weights, exact-versus-pooled support, unknown flags, row IDs, model version and resulting utility. Owner diagnostics may retain true binding IDs alongside these public predictions. Preserve full candidate projections, not only hashes/group extrema; the old traces demonstrate the limitation of the latter.

Log every newly admitted biological event once, its exposure binding, rates/at-risk increments changed, and the before/after predictions for the frozen query set. Log prediction use, not merely rows that become positive. Preserve nonbeneficial outcomes, stalled choices, censored endpoints and capacity misses.

Stopping condition for increment A: all invariant/resource/confounding/safety gates pass and the specified same-material prediction/rank change is established, or a gate fails/unavailable. Stop and report either outcome. Do not automatically run a seed sweep, add a planner, enlarge memory, change metabolism or promote to the default controller.

## 9. Research grounding and limits

[Barreto et al., 2017, *Successor Features for Transfer in Reinforcement Learning*](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/350db081a661525235354dd3e19b8c05-Abstract.html) motivates separating environment/outcome structure from reward weighting. Its assumptions and guarantees are not inherited by this partially observed, finite-evidence, changing-body experiment. The proposal above is not presented as an implementation of that algorithm.

[Kaplan and Meier, 1958, *Nonparametric Estimation from Incomplete Observations*](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452) establishes the importance of treating incomplete follow-up explicitly and states an independence assumption about observation loss. The proposed rate tables are a simpler engineered approximation, not Kaplan–Meier estimates. Policy-dependent proof loss/censoring may be informative; disclose it rather than claiming unbiased/calibrated survival probabilities.

The numerical shrinkage, finite-horizon use rule, support threshold, caps and pass thresholds here are new proposed engineering choices. No external code or licensed implementation is copied. The only implemented behavior analyzed is the existing frozen experiment.
