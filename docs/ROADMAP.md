# Aincrad roadmap

## Current advancement — 0.5

- Two persistent characters, Aster and Mira, with independent bodies, inventories, learned values, procedures and remembered maps.
- Preserved npm build/start/dev desktop workflow and original pixel presentation.
- Shared physical resources, saved day/weather clock and independent cave transitions.
- Qualitative bodily sensations in normal journal/model observations; exact physiology in explicit development inspection.
- Distinct energy, stamina, fatigue and sleep pressure, with timed rest/sleep, continued bodily costs and unsafe-condition interruptions.
- Concurrent Maslow-inspired survival, safety, belonging, competence, understanding, creativity, purpose and contribution drives.
- Bounded persistent beliefs and predictions with evidence, confidence and a distinction between observation, inference and reports.
- Restricted multi-action plans, carried-supply preparation, remembered return routes, travel budgets and interruption from actual outcomes.
- Local peer dialogue and familiarity, without exposing private memory or body values.
- An always-available human journal room with optional replies; unanswered questions do not block autonomous care.
- Persistent authored field notes that characters can encounter and read as reported knowledge.
- Shared local-model queue and budget with character-scoped cancellation; offline planning and dialogue remain available.
- Version 4 saves retaining Aster's earlier identity, memory and acquired water procedures while adding Mira.

The implementation supplies physiology, qualitative sensation mapping, drives, action affordances, navigation and bounded planning/dialogue/procedure templates. Observed geography, successful outcomes, evidence-backed procedures and experiential values arise during play. This distinction remains visible: the milestone is not a human brain simulation or proof of open-ended civilization.

## Evaluate and refine the two-character foundation

Measure how well both characters survive shared finite food, prepare for excursions, complete useful trips and return when conditions change. Track warning/critical time, injuries, meals/drinks, rests, sleep, shelter relief, route failures and collapses alongside discoveries and completed/interrupted plans. Assess resource renewal over longer runs and use more than one starting seed or weather condition.

For communication, measure useful knowledge exchange, report verification/correction, repeated messages and behavior while the human never replies. A successful conversation should be judged by later grounded decisions, not fluency alone. Compare offline templates with live Ollama behavior; deterministic provider fixtures do not establish a model's practical planning ability.

Improve anticipation from experienced travel and bodily changes, confidence calibration and plans that revise after failure. Broaden recovery and individual body differences only when the added systems create understandable consequences. Keep numeric physiology separate from felt awareness.

See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for the current verification record. A finite successful run does not establish optimal routes, unlimited survival or stable social development.

## Later ecological and social milestones

Add richer notes, knowledge revision, exchanging supplies and meaningful cooperative activities before expanding the population. Investigate voluntary specialization and shared projects rather than assigning everyone a profession at creation.

Hunting/fishing, farming, crafting, construction, player-assisted supplies/rescue, reproduction and larger societies require separate milestones. Physical construction would need validated world actions, material costs and observable outcomes; descriptions or intentions alone must never create buildings. Combat, multiplayer and sound remain outside the current scope.

The design draws conceptual inspiration from [Maslow Machines](https://github.com/agentciv/agentciv/blob/main/paper/maslow_machines.md), [TerraLingua](https://github.com/cognizant-ai-lab/terralingua) and [MicroPsi](https://github.com/joschabach/micropsi2). Their goals and results are not assumed to transfer automatically to Aincrad.
