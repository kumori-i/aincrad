# Aincrad roadmap

## Current advancement — 0.7 Cardinal and valley-wide habitats

- Persistent Cardinal supervision with private access to simulated bodies, outcomes, ecosystem state and finite-resource accounting.
- Saved bounded environmental rules and engine-actuated adjustments; no operating-system access or live executable source editing.
- Actual paired copied-world experiments with unchanged controls, training seeds and a held-out seed before automatic adoption.
- Persistent parameter/context utility estimates affect later proposal selection. Rejected candidates, decision reasons and interventions remain bounded evidence.
- Body/knowledge boundaries: Cardinal cannot invent human technique evidence, heal a character, refill an inventory or fabricate a successful action.
- Navigation-stall and accounting guards; outside assistance tracked separately from independent ecosystem production.
- Deterministic plant and animal habitats distributed across the traversable valley rather than clustered around the main spawn.
- Existing discovery saves gain Cardinal and missing habitats without discarding old object IDs, held material, bodies or memories. Legacy worlds retain their preceding physics and observation-only supervision.
- Zoomable field map with live Aster/Mira positions, including cave entrances when they are underground.

The supervisor's objective, actuators, safeguards and experiment policy are supplied structure. Its learned utility estimates summarize measured outcomes of environmental adjustments; they are not a learned general world model or LLM weight updates. Short held-out experiments do not establish indefinite ecological stability or civilization development. See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for actual validation horizons and limits.

## Retained foundation — 0.6 first food web

- Fresh discovery expeditions alongside preserved legacy saves.
- Opaque locally sensed objects, primitive physical interactions and evidence-based food discovery without supplied apple/forage/hunt recipes.
- Material-effect confidence separated from practiced technique proficiency; physical time, effort, reach and finite stock remain authoritative.
- Human instruction retained as an unverified reported hypothesis until physically tested.
- Approximate physiology in game hours/days, digestion, immediate/stored energy, water balance, circadian sleep pressure, limited sleeping perception and waking inertia.
- Finite seasonal grass, brambles and mature apple trees, detached materials, carcasses and decomposition.
- Rabbits, bobcats and foxes with rudimentary species-specific survival bodies and non-LLM local controllers.
- Animal feeding, pursuit, contact, escape and bounded reproduction consume actual resources.
- Discovery-mode local model use restricted to optional human-journal language; model actions, recipes, peer speech and notes cannot drive cold-start physical experiments.
- Independent Aster/Mira bodies, concurrent motives, persistent memory, local conversation and authored notes retained from 0.5.
- Continued simulation on focus loss, hiding and minimizing; only explicit Escape pause controls stop an open application. Map/help/journal remain live.
- Shared speed controls and saved simulation clock; no progression while the application is closed.
- Historical version 5 world snapshots, with old identities, techniques and legacy rules preserved during migration to the current format.

Movement, navigation, water handling, bodily sensation mapping, drives, primitive abilities, exploration and language templates are supplied structure. Discovery records which executed interactions affected the body and which techniques worked. This is not a blank human mind, neural simulation, clinical body model or proof of emergent civilization. Generated model language can contain pretrained associations even though the engine rejects its physical choices.

## Verify discovery, biology and ecological persistence

Use fresh expeditions to evaluate learning without food labels, menus, emergency recipes or hidden metadata. Compare changed appearances and IDs, investigate incorrect human advice, and check that unobserved reports remain uncertain. Measure actual attempts, delay to useful effects, technique reliability and proficiency gains; never reward mere claims or repeated failed loops.

Evaluate hunger, thirst, body condition, sleep loss, naps, waking and thermal strain over realistic game days. Sleeping actors must lose the awake visual stream while limited disturbances can wake them. Tests should distinguish awake time from elapsed time and resource consumption from externally supplied rescue.

Observe grass recovery, fruit seasons, grazing, predation, decomposition, population bounds and births across seasons. Match each consumer's gains to real resource losses and pursuit costs. A food web that survives a short run is not yet indefinitely sustainable. Divine recovery must not create infinite carcasses or outside energy disguised as ecological production.

Assess optional journal response behavior separately from physical discovery. Live Ollama language, deterministic provider fixtures and offline controller behavior are different checks. See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for completed runs and limits.

## Extend Cardinal only from measured limitations

Evaluate automatic versus observation-only worlds over longer intervals and independent seeds. Distinguish a useful short-horizon setting from one that merely postpones deprivation, depletes distant habitats or increases outside assistance. Evaluate continued capability with interventions disabled. Expand context features or objectives only when diagnostics show the missing factor.

Add new actuators through explicit engine contracts, bounded settings and physical accounting before exposing them to Cardinal. A future proposal to add fishing, construction or another mechanic must go through source development, tests and review; runtime supervision does not create executable mechanics. An optional language model can explain hypotheses, but cannot substitute an explanation for a completed paired experiment or measured outcome.

## Later food and technique milestones

Add roots/rhizomes, nuts, insects and eggs as distinct resources, then a finite aquatic food web with small prey organisms, fish growth/reproduction and harvest pressure. More plant variety should introduce different physical acquisition problems rather than arrive with an edible-item list.

Develop climbing through reachable supports, grip, movement and fall risk. Tool construction, traps, fishing methods, tissue processing and cooking need physically validated material transformations, costs and observable results. Farming requires seeds, growth conditions, land use and delayed yield. Descriptions of tools or meals must never create objects or outcomes.

After the first ecological loop is reliable, evaluate deer and larger predators with different diet, body size, threat and activity patterns. New animals should create measurable ecological roles rather than simple hostile decorations. Human hunting still requires discovered techniques and physical outcomes.

## Later cooperation and society

Expand knowledge correction, exchange of supplies and cooperative work before increasing the human population. Investigate voluntary specialization from practiced skills and needs, rather than assigning professions at birth. Construction, human reproduction and larger societies require separate milestones, validated actions and persistent material budgets.

The design draws conceptual inspiration from [Maslow Machines](https://github.com/agentciv/agentciv/blob/main/paper/maslow_machines.md), [TerraLingua](https://github.com/cognizant-ai-lab/terralingua) and [MicroPsi](https://github.com/joschabach/micropsi2). Their results are not assumed to transfer automatically to Aincrad.
