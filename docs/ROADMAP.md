# Current development roadmap

## Current single-world increment

- Direct-launch Ozark world; retire original-world routing and old-save compatibility.
- Original, richer pixel-art terrain, water and map presentation.
- Actual biome-appropriate trees/rocks with collision, sight, crown-derived canopy and solar shadows.
- Finite paid fishing integrated with safe carcass processing on the same map.
- Nearby attributed teaching of supported exact-source procedures; usable exact-target human advice.

These are engineering deliverables, not claims that a short free-running trial learns all skills. Required correctness, resource accounting, save safety, security, performance and native visual checks remain release gates. Ordinary behavior trials remain honestly reported diagnostics.

## Renewable ecology and sharing increment

- Bounded local aquatic production, feeding, resource-funded growth and parent-funded reproduction.
- Qualitative local food requests, voluntary exact-item offers and paid recipient handoff.

- Explicit long-run ecological and physical-transfer evidence, separately from autonomous adoption.

## Next separate capabilities

1. Broader habitat/ecological interactions and measured harvest capacity; no claim of a complete renewable nutrient cycle.
2. Practical interception/pursuit opportunities and reusable skills informed by observed failures, without endless priority tuning.
3. Food storage and processing/preservation, building on physical transfer without duplicated ownership or free stock.

5. Bounded Cardinal supervision integrated into the one current runtime.
6. Return to measured sustained living once the supporting capabilities exist.

## Known limits

Finite fishing's earlier three fixed 600-second autonomous trials did not produce catches. A controlled paid chain and native desktop persistence succeeded. The owner accepts capability delivery separately from adoption.

A controlled ordinary-power carcass trial succeeded at a stationary local exposure inside strike reach, outside the proximity-flee threshold. It does not establish pursuit. The paired unchanged-policy encounter did not kill or process prey.

No infinite food, complete nutrient cycle, calibrated animal anatomy, emergent society, humanlike feelings or indefinite survival is claimed. Older development reports document their historical source/fixtures and are not current runtime instructions.
