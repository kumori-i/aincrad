# Aincrad roadmap

## Next development: regional ecology and grounded adaptation

The first bounded regional ecological foundation is **implemented**, with [measured limitations](TESTING-regional-foundation.md). The sequence below is planned work, not a claim that autonomous relocation, sufficient nutrition or seasonal balance already works. [DEVELOPMENT-v08.md](DEVELOPMENT-v08.md) defines implementation boundaries, measurable acceptance gates and the evidence protocol. [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) retains the actual v0.7 results and limitations.

### 1. Regional ecological foundation — first bounded increment implemented

- Differentiate habitats using actual terrain, water access and climate/exposure, instead of repeating the same resource template throughout the valley.
- Introduce finite regional soil stocks and local production/decomposition/transfer accounting. Nutrient recycling and incoming photosynthetic energy remain distinct.
- Place sparse predators only where reachable prey/forage and water satisfy declared initial prerequisites. Verify population support later rather than treating placement as proof.
- Audit traversable habitat corridors and water banks with physical collision/radius checks. Add bounded vegetated stepping stones in fresh worlds where appropriate; audit travel and food-access costs separately.
- Migrate regional metadata/stock allocation without replacing objects, people, memories, held material or depletion. Old depleted worlds must never be refilled; repeated loads must be idempotent.

Gate: deterministic differentiated placement, local stock/transfer tests, corridor diagnostics, conservation, privacy and migration checks. This increment does not claim a completed movement/learning policy or sustainable food web.

### 2. Caloric throughput and sequence learning — correctness increment implemented

The first [bounded correctness increment](TESTING-discovery-throughput.md) repairs learned retry loops and overlapping-feedback attribution, with explicitly isolated positive-food-association controls. Its six- and twelve-hour paired intake results are mixed, not a survival or efficiency improvement. Cost-aware learning and the gates below remain planned.

Trace encounter → manipulation → detached material → swallowed portion → delayed benefit. Measure absorbed/spent calories, reserves, water, sequence interruptions, failed loops, carry/digestion limits, sleep and travel costs. Diagnose why a useful taste fails to become adequate intake before increasing food supply.

Develop bounded reuse/selection of physically experienced action sequences with delayed credit assignment. Compare the unchanged controller, learning-enabled candidate, clearly defined learning-disabled control and, where applicable, trained-but-frozen policy. All retain the same senses, primitives and physical costs.

Gate: frozen multi-seed development/held-out tests, remote starts across terrain and all quadrants, no injected knowledge, and at least seven-day evidence of materially better acquisition. The detailed plan sets a final-three-day caloric-balance target and a paired learning-control improvement gate. Keep failures visible; remaining initial reserves do not prove self-care.

### 3. Threat-informed relocation and physical animal movement — planned

Learn risk from locally observed pursuit, contact, pain and disturbance. Choose remembered alternatives or explore visible frontiers without supplied safe destinations, remote resource knowledge or teleportation. Arrival must be followed by actual food/water acquisition before calling a move viable.

Move animals physically across regions with travel costs and stable identities. Record resident movement, births, deaths and outside immigration separately. Introducing another predator is an external addition, not proof of migration or reproduction.

Gate: matched no-threat/threat/disconnected-path fixtures, valid local knowledge and paths, measured threat/injury reduction, and at least one day of post-arrival self-care in declared solvable scenarios.

### 4. Bounded regional Cardinal interventions — planned

Extend only existing tested engine mechanisms. Each regional intervention needs prerequisites, affected area, magnitude, finite duration, resource or explicit external cost, cooldown, concurrency bound, monitoring and rollback rules. Diagnose navigation/learning failures independently of ecological scarcity.

Use unchanged paired controls, multiple training pairs and untouched held-out regional conditions. Screen briefly, then promote only after appropriate two-day, seven-day or seasonal tests. Compare adaptive, observation-only and frozen-policy worlds, including an intervention-off continuation. Rollback stops/reverses settings; it cannot undo deaths, consumed resources or elapsed time.

Gate: real measured benefit to the diagnosed problem without hidden imports, adjacent-region depletion or body/knowledge manipulation. Preserve rejected evidence. Cardinal cannot add executable mechanics or rewrite source at runtime.

### 5. Seasonal persistence and local laptop validation — planned

Extend evaluation from 7/30-day diagnostics to a complete 120-day ecological year, then two cycles on selected untouched scenarios. Track resource/soil trajectories, populations, births, deaths, predation costs, migration and outside assistance. Declare the populations and processes expected to persist; local extinction and barren regions must not be hidden by summaries.

One game day is 3,600 simulation seconds. Current 900-second Cardinal experiments cover only six game hours, one quarter-day. One year is 432,000 seconds at the normal calendar rate. A short successful fixture is not a seasonal test, and finite seasonal evidence is not indefinite sustainability.

Keep runtime and any behavior training local, offline-capable and CPU-feasible on varied 32 GB RAM / 1 TB laptops without assuming a dedicated GPU. Benchmark real hardware, bound jobs/memory/logs, and leave headroom for the OS. Optional Ollama language remains separate from learned physical behavior and must never become a required cloud or paid-API dependency.

Gate: frozen-source longitudinal evidence, zero unaccounted creation/refills, checkpoint/restart and timestep checks, and measured CPU/memory/frame/persistence budgets on named laptops. Container timing alone does not establish laptop performance.

### 6. New mechanics, then cooperation and society — later

Develop one material mechanism at a time in isolated source-tested copies: additional plants/invertebrates, finite aquatic ecology, climbing, processing, tools, traps, cooking, farming and construction. Require physical inputs, outputs, costs, failure cases, local observations, conservation and migration tests before promotion to the live application.

Then evaluate uncertain knowledge exchange, finite-supply exchange and cooperative work against matched individual controls. Specialization must arise from practiced capability and needs rather than assigned professions. Human reproduction and larger societies need separate milestones. Dialogue, invented plans and authored role labels do not establish material cooperation or civilization.

Keep implementation original. Do not copy EVAAA noncommercial/share-alike code into Aincrad; check licenses before any external reuse. Research ideas are hypotheses to test, not inherited evidence of success.

## Retained v0.7 foundation: Cardinal and the atlas

- Persistent private Cardinal diagnostics, bounded saved environmental settings and authoritative engine actuation.
- Real paired copied-world experiments, training/held-out continuations, retained rejection reasons and learned parameter/context utility estimates.
- Bodies, inventories, empirical technique evidence and private memories outside the supervisor's write boundary.
- Navigation-stall and accounting guards; outside assistance distinct from independent production.
- Deterministic valley-wide ecology and a zoomable/pannable map showing the player, Aster and Mira, including cave entrances for underground characters.
- Preserved historical save identities/progress and legacy-mode physics. The historical v0.7 distribution migration added missing habitat resources once and recorded external energy; the new regional migration must not repeat that additive behavior.

The supervisor's objectives, candidate actuators and experiment policy are supplied structure. Persisted utility estimates summarize measured outcomes, not LLM weight training or a general world model. Current short held-out continuations reuse checkpoint geometry; they are not independent newly generated maps. See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for the actual evidence.

## Retained v0.6 foundation: the first food web

- Opaque locally observed objects, generic physical primitives and evidence-based discovery without supplied food labels or acquisition recipes.
- Separate material-effect confidence and practice, with time, effort, reach, ownership and finite stock enforced.
- Human/peer reports remain uncertain until tested; words and plans do not count as actions.
- Approximate game-hour/day physiology, delayed digestion, immediate/stored energy, water balance, circadian sleep pressure, vulnerable sleep and waking inertia.
- Finite grass, brambles, mature apple trees, detached material, carcasses, decomposition, rabbits, bobcats and foxes.
- Physical feeding, pursuit/contact, flight and resource-costed bounded reproduction.
- Independent Aster/Mira bodies, concurrent motives, persistent memory, local conversation, authored notes and optional human journal.
- Optional local model restricted to journal language in discovery mode; it cannot choose cold-start physical experiments or supply executable recipes.
- Shared saved simulation time, live background/minimized operation, explicit Escape pause, and no progression while closed.

Movement, local navigation, water handling, sensation mapping, motives, primitive abilities and exploration structure are supplied. These mechanisms are not a blank human mind, clinical biology, neural simulation or proof of emergent civilization. Pretrained model language may contain associations even when physical actions reject it.

## Evidence before promotion

Freeze source/configuration/evaluator/checkpoint fingerprints, predeclare thresholds and held-out conditions, preserve every trial including failures, and rerun mixed-source evidence. Separate functional fixtures, native application QA, long biological runs, local-model tests and real-hardware benchmarks. State what is implemented, what was measured and what remains unrun.

Conceptual inspirations already documented include Maslow Machines, TerraLingua and MicroPsi; see [architecture.md](architecture.md). Their implementations or results are not assumed to transfer to Aincrad.
