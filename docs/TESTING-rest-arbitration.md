# Remembered rest refusals and bounded care

This increment remembers an actual refused/interrupted rest, briefly reconsiders it using qualitative bodily changes, and permits personally learned care while depleted. It preserves physical rest eligibility, metabolism, stores, action costs, digestion, food supply and sensory thresholds. Fewer rejected requests alone are not physiological recovery.

## Corrected diagnosis

The previous twelve-game-hour development lakeshore trial ended during a temporary refusal spell. Continuing the unchanged controller at native 1/60-second steps finds the first energy-related refusal at 1,713.42 simulation seconds, successful sleep beginning at 1,811.42, and its completion at 3,011.43. The original horizon was 1,800 seconds. The spell therefore lasts 98 seconds and resolves without any new food: existing stores mobilize energy faster than awake basal use. Later cold exposure produces a separate refusal episode. This is not an established permanent energy deadlock.

The developer-only eligibility calculation is 112 available kcal; the inhabitant's qualitative depleted sensation spans a wider range. The policy never receives that numeric eligibility test. Two apparently similar depleted checkpoints can therefore have very different time-to-rest and feeding/rest trade-offs.

## Bounded mechanism

- Retain the latest real rest/sleep outcome in the bounded action history, including a later success. Unrelated successful actions cannot erase the refusal or resurrect an older failure. No new save field, executable state or independent timer is introduced.
- A refused or interrupted rest is reconsidered after at most 60 simulation seconds (24 game minutes), or sooner after improvement in an existing safety-related qualitative sensation. Irrelevant activity, fatigue, sleepiness and transient relief do not clear the gate. An actually observed scene change can prompt an earlier thermal retry. Missing legacy evidence is conservative; invalid saved thermal values cannot crash the controller.
- Existing contact escape, thirst care and observed thermal refuge priorities remain ahead of depleted food care. While feeling depleted after the refusal, a personally supported held piece or an already earned acquisition/sampling sequence can be tried before resting. Nearby observed sources precede remote ones; a source without a usable observed route cannot conceal another reachable source.
- A physically acquired, currently held, not-yet-sampled result of an earned sequence may finish one cautious first trial, even if a save clears its transient run. Exact retained acquisition and source/operation evidence are required. The new piece is not labeled nourishing or safe, and its normal pending wait, fullness, failure and aversion gates remain. This prevents an older supported piece or sequence expiry from stranding an actual unfinished acquisition.
- When there is no eligible learned care and no competing acute observed danger, a short authoritative wait conserves activity expenditure before reassessment. Empty gut and absent stores do not manufacture recovery. Rest never becomes permanently prohibited, and the physical engine can still reject an early retry. Active thermal danger or desperate thirst with no usable remembered refuge/water can still fall through to exploration; this increment does not guarantee safe or efficient search in those cases.

This remains supplied goal arbitration around personally learned consequences, not a newly trained general policy. Feeding can delay imminent rest by spending energy even while increasing eventual intake. Source learning, held-piece benefit and rest safety are separate evidence domains.

## Verification and limits

The regression suite covers actual refusal/interruption, finite expiry, meaningful bodily improvement, failure-history rotation, full save/reopen, malformed/legacy snapshots, genuinely impossible empty-store care, finite learned intake, source exhaustion, actual observed navigation, two-source fallback, unfinished acquisition, physical digestive capacity, ablation, real refuge entry and predator contact.

All 372 source tests pass, including 20 new rest-arbitration regressions. Build, [actual Linux Electron smoke](rest-arbitration/native-smoke.json) and [two-process disk reopen](rest-arbitration/native-reopen.json) pass at the frozen source fingerprint. Independent read-only review cleared the runtime and related regressions. The native disk test closes and reopens an isolated Electron profile, preserving both the complete checkpoint and a real refused-rest outcome across an unrelated successful wait. No physical laptop or live Ollama performance is claimed.

Remaining limitations include broad qualitative signals, the feeding/rest trade-off, generic novelty's cost-blind strikes, exact-instance learning, limited evidence history and inadequate long-term food throughput. This increment is not proof of caloric balance, seven-day self-care, seasonal ecology, viable migration or predator-pressure adaptation. Delayed harmful effects remain a risk for learned consumption; a nourishing experience does not establish safety.
