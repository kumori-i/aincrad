## Bounded supplied rule

Two own walking ACTION_TIMEOUT receipts may temporarily defer the same observed directed tile approach. Each action must reach the normal 12-second deadline within 0.05 seconds, pay at least 8 seconds at its final waypoint, and improve its remaining waypoint distance by at most 20 units. The two approach origins, the two waypoint targets, and the two completion positions must each be within 40 units of their counterpart. Paid time at earlier waypoints cannot be pooled. Same-tile approaches are excluded because a tile self-edge cannot distinguish reversal.

Evidence lives only in actor-local WeakMaps, not saves or persisted blockedEdges. At most eight records are retained, with two receipts needed to activate a direction. Both receipt objects must remain in that actor's retained experience and each must be less than 60 seconds old. The actor must remain within 160 units of the latest approach origin. Scene, epoch, discovery identity, clock rollback, receipt loss, elapsed time and displacement revoke evidence. A successful move clears incomplete pairs. Reset, cloning and save/reopen cannot recreate runtime authority.

The navigation graph, initial anchor approach and remembered resource final tail honor only the failed direction. Reverse travel remains eligible. Urgent hunger, thirst, depleted energy, severe pain, dangerous thermal conditions and recent personally experienced contact bypass the preference. Swim/climb admission is unchanged. No hidden world, current, food, species or nutrition knowledge is consulted, and no reward or save schema is changed. The default null-directed-evidence route behavior and frozen oracle remain unchanged.

This is a supplied retry heuristic, not a learned abstract navigation concept. Repeatedly slow positive progress of at most 20 units can still qualify. Deferral may leave no currently known route; expiration permits another attempt. Neither alternative passability nor sustainable survival is guaranteed.
