## Exact dual-checkout, once-only execution

The dedicated workflow checks out baseline at its full pinned commit and candidate
at the activated reviewed SHA into separate roots. Both checkouts use
`persist-credentials:false`. Coordinator verifies exact heads/trees, clean tracked
files, no credential-helper/http-extraheader/URL-rewrite configuration and expected
credential-free remote URLs. Neither .git contents nor environment secrets are
copied into artifacts. Simulator/analyzer subprocesses receive an explicit small
nonsecret environment allowlist, excluding tokens and Git credential overrides.

Publishing files is inert. Execution requires separately approved creation of
`experiments/motor-recall-ordinary-v1-once`, attempt1. The read-only guard checks
complete filtered AND unfiltered dedicated-workflow history, rejecting older or
recreated activations, malformed/incomplete pagination and missing current-run
indexing. No workflow_dispatch, rerun, branch recreation or automatic retry.
Concurrency does not cancel an existing job.

## Minimal observations and integrity

Each successful tick checks actor/world/incarnation/epoch/memory/discovery/body
scope. Paid receipt IDs are captured without gaps or silent mutation while
retained. Append-only JSONL keeps receipts, one-second public snapshots, changed
procedure choices/inquiry status and candidate motor-recall status transitions.
Progress markers and terminal output distinguish completed and partial runs.
Initial state and final valid save are separate raw files, not per-tick saves.
Abrupt termination can leave a truncated final journal line; that remains partial.
No durability or continuation-equivalence guarantee is inferred.

Initial state must match after detached normalization of exactly four equal UUID
bindings: worldLineage.instanceId, visualReference.worldId,
characterMessages.worldId and playerLessons.worldId. Raw bytes/hashes are retained.
No actor/evidence IDs, RNG, observations, physiology or outcomes are normalized.
Final states may differ because policy differs. Result/journal/raw-save hashes,
source pins, scope/receipt completeness and completed ticks are mandatory.

## Interpretation funnel, not an automatic success assertion

Report separately:
1. Observed paid pull followed by a same-source physical grasp-failure motif.
2. Actual candidate question admission from the private status projection.
3. Recall selection narration, only as a candidate attribution clue.
4. Observed canonical `pulling` phase linked to the same question/source.
5. Matching paid action and actual detached/rejected/interrupted consequence.

Baseline motifs are **potential exposure**, never reconstructed eligibility.
Public data cannot establish every private witness property. Method/admission
counts are not executions. A matching ordinary pull without canonical evidence
receives no recall credit. A narration alone is not execution. Latest-status
snapshots can miss within-tick transitions; absent phases remain unknown. The
analyzer organizes candidate links for independent review and never labels them
confirmed recalled actions automatically.

Outside-food classification uses only the actor's complete trace: prior ingestion
or positive feedback on the exact source and its personally produced material
chain. It checks admission and, when observed, through first canonical pulling.
Unobserved pulling means that later classification stays unknown. Absence is asserted only when the initial actor has empty experiment/effect
ledgers and zero prior ingested calories; otherwise initial food history remains
unknown. Other meals elsewhere during this run are not source-specific food evidence. No species/edibility labels enter
policy or substitute for this provenance test. Raw chain/receipt IDs are retained
for independent inspection, including later same-source food evidence.

Report paid seconds, physical stocks/materials, handling effort, risk and actual
failures separately from sampled action occupancy. Whole-body energy, water,
health/care differences provide context, not a claim that all changes were caused
by recall or that recall is beneficial. A single pair cannot establish transfer,
reliable survival, learned rule invention or generalization. Authored engineering
checks are not spontaneous ordinary validation.

### Semantic evidence admission

Hashes bind retained bytes, not payload completeness. Analysis separately requires
all actors at initial time, each one-second boundary, and the duplicate terminal
capture (1202 snapshots per actor), finite physical fields, qualitative body
fields, actor-scoped ordered receipt IDs/times, ordered choice/inquiry times,
recall tick/time agreement, and exactly 1200 sampled occupancy seconds per actor.
Initial and terminal snapshot physical projections agree with their respective
actor states. The terminal agent/save projection is explicitly limited to id,
elapsed, position, lifecycle, RNG, and recorded physiology fields (energy stores,
water deficit, health, condition, stamina, fatigue, sleep pressure, totals and
outside assistance). It does not equate ephemeral runtime authority with saves.

Material ancestry joins successful physical pull receipts across **all** actors;
consumption and positive feedback still belong only to the actor being analyzed.
This includes another actor's material subsequently handed over. Missing or
conflicting provenance is unknown, not outside-food evidence. No species label
supplies an ancestry or food classification. Contract tests preserve literal
historical source pins and wrong-source rejection without requiring future
unrelated checkout trees to remain equal to this cohort's candidate runtime.

Initial world and every actor clock must be zero; terminal world and actor clocks
must equal the fixed horizon. Missing parent links, ancestry cycles, incompatible
parents and consumed objects outside the proven descendant chain remain unknown.
A negative also requires the queried source to be a unique physical plant/animal
ID in the initial ecology, with no observed parent; newly produced or unresolved
sources cannot receive a negative classification. This uses physical identity,
not species labels. Ambiguous ancestry takes precedence over apparent positive chain evidence.

Zero observed questions is not proof of zero admissions: latest-status capture can
miss within-tick phases or scope retirement. No-prior-food classifications describe
retained evidence only, not the actor's motivation.
