# Tentative local method transfer

This bounded increment lets an earned acquisition → sample procedure guide one trial on a different, locally seen source. It is not a general planner, learned pursuit, autonomous fishing, an edibility classifier, or a claim of humanlike social learning.

## What was supplied and what was experienced

The supplied action grammar remains `pull → sample` or `grasp → sample`. A sender can report it only after retaining its own successful acquisition, its own actual sample, and matching delayed sustained bodily feedback. The sender did not invent these primitive verbs or the two-step representation. Tests may deliberately expose a teacher and request its training actions; that demonstrates evidence retention and subsequent communication, not autonomous discovery of the training sequence.

The supplied appearance abstraction is exact equality of three visible strings: shape, color, and texture. These are not species, food labels, stock, nutrients, hidden properties, safety, or even a guarantee that the same action works. In particular, flowering or immature appearances may differ; identical bark does not prove fruit is present. The matching procedure is engineered, not a discovered general concept.

Only a current runtime sensory receipt less than eight simulation seconds old, in the same scene and line of sight, supplies the sender's appearance cues. Saved observations alone cannot manufacture this receipt. Speech is regenerated from that verified method and bounded observation, not arbitrary tagged prose. It contains no recipient target or navigation coordinates.

## Recipient admission

A fresh reachable exact source keeps the existing exact-source teaching behavior. Otherwise, when the sender supplies fresh appearance cues, the listener gets one matching opportunity for that sender/source/acquisition combination. All currently seen matching sources within physical reach are counted before checking stock, affordability, outcomes, or availability. Exactly one different identity is required. Zero or multiple matches leave an unavailable attributed report, without an oracle explanation. No remote approach or target substitution is part of cross-source transfer.

A unique match permits one paid acquisition and one sample of its actual result. The ordinary action engine still checks geometry, ownership, capacity, finite stock, time, effort and prerequisites. Full hands, urgent bodily care, and prior adverse personal feedback take precedence. Adverse detached-material feedback is traced to its actual acquired source, not only the material ID. Similar appearance cannot override that evidence.

The matching opportunity is consumed even when no candidate can be bound. Later sightings, rewritten speech, a new message ID or teacher technique ID, report compaction, and restart cannot refresh it. A non-evicting admission ledger holds at most 64 stable sender/source/acquisition identities per recipient. At saturation, only new cross-source admissions stop; ordinary discovery, care and exact-source teaching remain available. This conservative lifetime bound avoids memory-compaction retry loops.

Conversation transfers no food, XP, proficiency, acquisition receipt or personal outcome. Heard methods, witnessed consumption inquiries and personally tested outcomes remain separate. A receiver cannot relay an untested method as its own established technique. A genuine personal sample can support, contradict, or leave a report inconclusive; no noticeable benefit does not renew its trial allowance.

## Persistence and authority

Schema 12 and `aincrad.world.v2` are unchanged. Optional bounded `methodTrials` and teaching `transfer` fields are strict data. Old exact-source records retain their existing behavior. The teacher's source and receiver's bound target are separate IDs. Restore reconstructs progress only from the receiver's matching acquisition, material, sample and bodily-feedback receipts. A restarted unfinished action is cancelled rather than replayed or debited twice. A retained pre-acquisition report still needs a new live local sighting before the policy can act.

New current-save metadata is checked rather than silently repaired. Malformed cues, unknown fields, impossible times, unsupported action/effect links or invalid admission rows protect the save. Model observation IPC permits only the bounded report representation and does not grant model physical actions, code execution or hidden ecology access.

## Evaluation interpretation

Teacher retention and recipient transfer must be assessed separately. Matched recipient comparisons hold teacher experience, receiver body/resources, geometry and encounter fixed. Removing usable communication changes information, which is the intended treatment. Such a comparison can demonstrate use of a message, not superiority over an ordinary message carrying the same information. A stronger claim would require an information-matched control.

The fixtures use distinct naturally generated source IDs and locations and normal paid physical actions. Their manually arranged exposure and teacher training are explicit. Finite controller diagnostics are reported as observed, including any control success, rather than tuned until the transfer condition wins. See the accompanying verification report for actual outcomes and native-platform limits.
