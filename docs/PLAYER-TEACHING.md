# Player food demonstrations

The player now has a physical way to demonstrate one food action in the main Ozark world. Walk within sight and reach of a ripe apple tree, bramble, or loose fruit. **Gather** takes real time and effort, removing one finite portion only when the work completes. **Eat sample** swallows part of a held portion; the avatar's gut receives its actual nutrients and digestion runs with world time. **Drop rest** leaves the remaining portion in the world. Moving, pausing, opening a panel, or losing the window interrupts pending work; time and effort already paid are not refunded. A pending action is not saved or resumed.

This is a chance for an agent to learn, not a command to learn. Aster or Mira must personally see the completed consumption and its source. Even then, observation opens only an uncertain inquiry about that exact remaining piece. The agent must independently choose, travel, pay for acquisition and sampling, and experience its own delayed bodily result. Occlusion, distance, an unavailable remainder, competing needs, a failed swallow, or an agent's prior adverse history can prevent a useful trial. Nothing copies the player's nutrition result or intent into an agent. This milestone does not demonstrate autonomous teaching success or category transfer between different apples or berries.

The player can demonstrate berries as well as apples, but a berry is small. A 30 g sample from a 35 g berry leaves only 5 g; an apple leaves a larger remainder for an agent's own test. The material must remain reachable after the player drops it.

## Next grounded language step

The next proposed bridge is **typed, in-world utterances** made while the player and an object or action are actually visible to a nearby agent. The utterance would be recorded as an audible/visible event with a bounded time and location, then paired only with that agent's own contemporaneous perception and later experience. Words would not be translated into hidden object identities, recipes, food safety, rewards, or commands. The agent would have to learn any association from repeated local evidence; no English dictionary, pretrained interpreter, Ollama, or imported checkpoint is part of this plan. This input bridge is not yet implemented.

Speaking aloud is a later interface question. Microphone permission, speech transcription, cloud upload and voice learning are **not** part of this milestone. A microphone button without a grounded event and learner would not teach language, so typed input is the first testable implementation choice. Swimming imitation is also later work, after the food demonstration path has native evidence and its limits are clear.
