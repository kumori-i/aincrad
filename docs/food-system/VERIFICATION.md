## Native scope and remaining limits

Prepare-only runners and Node serialization checks passed. No actual Electron
window, native screenshot or separate native process reopen was executed for
these local results. Supported-platform CI must record its actual architecture,
source/build identity, strict sandbox, normal close and two reopens.

The minimal runner captures whole-food and water-only UI states, tests immediate
partial-dose/water values numerically, and compares true pre-save physical state
with reopened state. The screenshot-state follows the final bite by0.2s; the
immediate projection is a separate numeric check.1/2/3-hour curves are engine
checks, not hourly native screenshots. `fullChecklistAcceptance:false` preserves
that limited coverage. RESTART_INTERRUPTED has no paid-duration field; measured
pre-save paid work and preserved debit/no replay are checked instead.

[Historical evidence](HISTORICAL-EVIDENCE.md) retains failed budget/harm tests and
ordinary actor regressions. Appetite satisfaction is not energy sufficiency;
self-sustaining food discovery or autonomous survival is not established.
