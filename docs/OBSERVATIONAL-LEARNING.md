# Observation-driven inquiry: first learning cycle

## Purpose

Connect witnessed events to an uncertain question, an actual experiment, and revision from the observer's own outcomes. This is a small psychology-inspired functional cycle, not a model of brain anatomy, human-level creativity, or reliable autonomous living.

The first slice concerns consumption. It must use a real accepted eating event, not an animal's activity label, proximity to food, a journal sentence, or hidden dietary information. Existing world behavior supplies the opportunities; this increment does not implement bears catching fish.

## Evidence boundaries

- A witness sees a local actor consume a particular visible object. The observer must be awake and able to see the actor and target when the event occurs. Later proximity cannot reconstruct a missed event.
- Observation motivates a question about the source; it does not prove human benefit, safety, calories, proficiency, or ownership.
- An experiment uses the observer's own physical actions, finite stocks, capacity, reach, visibility, handling time and effort. Seeing someone hold or consume material does not permit stealing it or manufacturing a copy.
- Failure to acquire material is not evidence that the material is nutritionally useless. Unavailable, deferred and physically failed attempts remain distinct from bodily evidence.
- A successful sample is not itself proof of benefit. Only the observer's matching delayed bodily evidence can revise the relevant expectation. Water relief is not nutritional support; adverse evidence must remain visible even alongside benefit.
- Retained observational testimony and hypotheses stay separate from firsthand acquisition, ingestion, practice and learned technique proofs. Reload cannot replay completed work or turn an interrupted attempt into success.

The saved witnessed record is structurally checked for bounded fields, times and geometry; it is not cryptographic authentication against an attacker rewriting an internally consistent entire history. Own nutritional and practice proofs retain their existing independent receipt requirements.

## Functional cycle

1. Perception admits an actual visible consumption event.
2. Bounded attention selects a relevant inquiry without displacing urgent care or escape.
3. Memory retains who/what was observed, uncertainty, and the observer's own subsequent evidence.
4. A question motivates a feasible experiment using available motor primitives.
5. An action executes through the ordinary physical controller.
6. Its result and later matching bodily feedback revise the inquiry.

The initial acquisition-and-sampling experiment is supplied general scaffolding, not an invented plan. The learner does not yet synthesize a complete chase/strike/catch/process/eat strategy or generalize an exact source to every similar object. Questions must change action selection rather than merely decorate dialogue.

## Verification standard

Use matched seeded encounters with and without a genuine witness. Attribute the selected experiment to the inquiry; unobserving controls may still perform ordinary exploration, so a globally inactive control is not required. Include occluded, sleeping, out-of-range, late-arriving, unsuccessful-consumption and nonconsumption controls.

Check real finite acquisition and sampling, benefit/adverse revision from matching personal evidence, unavailable ownership, full capacity, urgent-care deferral, bounded retention, and strict save tamper/interruption behavior. Native Linux/macOS evidence must separately cover rendered inquiry state and actual close/reopen persistence.

Prepared encounters establish the mechanism, not spontaneous discovery from ordinary spawn, successful fishing/hunting, net nutritional profitability, or indefinite survival. Report failed and unrun diagnostics plainly.

## Current limits and persistence

The initial attention budget retains at most 16 exact-source inquiries and admits new experiments for 300 unpaused simulation seconds after the witnessed event. A generic scaffold tries grasp, then (if needed) pull, and samples actual owned material. It does not route to a distant source, infer an unseen source, learn interception, or transfer a technique across object identities. An unresolved sample cannot monopolize observation-led attention after that window.

Schema 12 and the current v2 slot remain in use. Saves without the optional inquiry field load with no inquiries. Malformed inquiry fields or unsupported own-action links fail closed. If a save indicates any interrupted action, all unfinished observational inquiries are conservatively retired because the canonical disk record does not retain enough active-action identity to identify just one. Existing completed samples and their own bodily evidence are preserved. This can abandon a useful unfinished question; it does not replay work or invent a result.

The physical transaction must complete even if a consumption listener fails. Such faults are recorded as bounded runtime diagnostics rather than interrupting ingestion after the finite source debit.

## Later work

Keep perception, inquiry state, candidate selection and feedback interpretation separate so multiple questions can eventually compete or be evaluated concurrently. Parallel execution, broad causal generalization, learned multi-step planning, language acquisition and tool invention are outside this first increment.
