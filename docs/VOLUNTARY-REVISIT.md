# One optional remembered-place walk

This increment fills a specific gap: generic object approaches previously used
only observations younger than eight seconds. Food procedures can route to older
known sources, but motor recall and producer inquiry require a local source;
connected sequences only prefer a follow-up after a new output already exists.
This is not a claim that the gap caused the frozen ordinary cohort's sparse
repeated contexts, nor a guarantee of more qualifying episodes.

An actor's own paid successful grasp/pull/strike/catch plus actual ordinary sight
can retain one place association. It retains references to the original personal
experiment/receipt, not copies credited as new evidence or retention owners.
Private scope binds actor, epoch, containers, environment and world. Only actual
observations can retire a moved, replaced or possessed source. The remote chooser
never looks up its current location, stock, health or existence. A stale place may
be empty. Save/reload preserves no live association or movement authority.

At most16 associations are held. Between20 and300 seconds after the encounter,
with no sight of that source in the last8 seconds, a safe actor may consider one
known-map waypoint toward the remembered place. The goal may be up to900 units
away; the selected observed route is at most480 units, rechecked at dispatch.
Movement is at most8 seconds; further voluntary work stops at a slice boundary
once actual travel reaches480 units. Current-driven displacement is not a promise
of an exact spatial stop. This is one waypoint, not a
multistep pursuit or a promise to reach the source. No automatic handling,
inspection, A/B replay, reward, episode, food value or arrival proof follows.
Future ordinary perception and the normal chooser determine the next action.

The authored optional-curiosity prior is5 minus (goal distance + waypoint route
length)/1500. It adds no RNG draws. Earlier handling is the admission evidence,
not a penalty for having already answered a return question. Applying the old
approach's total-handling-attempt penalty made a newly earned return necessarily
lose to an unvisited terrain option; that draft and its failing authored chooser
control are retained privately. The explicit revised prior is not learned utility
or an outcome-tuned reward. Fresh ordinary body operations and all earlier care,
procedure and safety decisions retain their own competition/priority.

Only the owner’s recognizable selected dispatch spends the source, including
stale-proof/time/scope or care/route rejection. A spent attempt is not a
successful walk. Cross-actor misuse cannot spend either actor’s budget.
No menu enumeration spends it. At most16 sources per actor incarnation may be
spent. A strict optional saved `discovery.revisitSpent` array carries only these
exclusions, never permissions. Eviction, epoch changes and reload cannot renew a
spent source. A20-second inter-dispatch cooldown is additional to each place's
minimum age. Exact issued-request replay, cross-actor forwarding, stale proof and
invalid scope fail closed rather than silently becoming unguarded ordinary moves.
Independently authored ordinary movement remains ordinary movement.

Selection, dispatch and each movement slice require settled qualitative body
signals plus explicit hunger, thirst, pain, stamina, fatigue, digestion, sleep and
thermal checks. Physical routing, effort, collisions and normal failures remain
unchanged. A blocked or interrupted attempt is spent; it cannot automatically
retry. Detached status reads allocate no shared evidence IDs and consume no RNG.

Authored controls deliberately arrange experiences and known geometry. They are
engineering evidence, not autonomous adoption, hunting, survival improvement,
comparative ranking or generalization evidence. Frozen scientific cohorts and
protocols are unchanged and are not rerun for this increment.
