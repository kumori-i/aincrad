Survival & communication

The same Aster and Mira learn from their own physical experience and exchange symbols while surviving here. This view does not create a separate world.

Bodies, survival memories and acquired visual associations save locally per individual. Valid saves restore each continuing individual’s associations after app reopen. Unfinished gestures and session events do not resume. Reset world starts bodies and memories fresh.
